DhunPad privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

DhunPad is a music app for Android for playing drums, tabla, piano and guitar, making beats and recording takes. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

DhunPad does not collect, store on any server, sell or share your personal data. Everything you make stays on your phone.

Microphone

DhunPad asks for microphone access only when you switch on the Mic to record your voice or an instrument over a take, or to measure your phone's audio delay (calibration). The audio is processed and saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. It is never uploaded or sent anywhere. You can turn the Mic off at any time, or remove the permission in your phone's settings.

Your recordings

Takes, beats and settings are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include your takes, beats and settings (not microphone audio) in your device backup, which Google handles under your Google account settings. The developer has no access to it.

When you export or share a WAV file, it goes only where you choose to send it, for example a messaging app or Files.

Internet, analytics and ads

DhunPad does not use the internet. It has no analytics or tracking, no advertising and no account sign-in. Google Play may give the developer anonymous crash and performance statistics (Android vitals) if you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics with Google.

Children

DhunPad does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, for example by adding optional ads or purchases, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
